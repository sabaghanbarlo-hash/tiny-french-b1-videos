VOCABULARY
le parcours (luh par-KOOR) - background, career path
naître (NEH-truh) - to be born
grandir (grahn-DEER) - to grow up
à l'âge de (ah LAHZH duh) - at the age of
quitter (kee-TAY) - to leave
obtenir (ohb-tuh-NEER) - to obtain, to get
le bac (luh BAHK) - baccalauréat (school-leaving exam)
marquer (mar-KAY) - to leave a mark on, to affect
décider de (day-see-DAY duh) - to decide to

GRAMMAR
Telling a life story - Use the passé composé for events that moved your life forward and the imparfait for what was going on around them. Naître is conjugated with être, and the participle agrees: elle est née, ils sont nés. Grandir is regular with avoir: j'ai grandi.
Dates and ages - Say the year with en and the month with en or au mois de. For a precise day: le 3 mars. For age, use à l'âge de plus number plus ans, or à plus number plus ans: à seize ans. Use il y a to place an event from today: il y a dix ans.
