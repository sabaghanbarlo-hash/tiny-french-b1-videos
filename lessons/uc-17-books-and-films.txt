title: Talking About Books and Films
episode: UC17
scene: cafe

[story]
Adrien: Alors, le film dont tout le monde parle, il t'a plu ? | So, the film everyone is talking about, did you like it?
Marinette: Beaucoup ! C'est l'histoire d'un boulanger qui décide de changer de vie. | A lot! It's the story of a baker who decides to change his life.
Adrien: C'est le réalisateur dont tu m'avais parlé ? | Is it the director you told me about?
Marinette: Oui. Il a aussi adapté un roman que j'ai lu l'an dernier. | Yes. He also adapted a novel that I read last year.
Adrien: Et le personnage principal, il est comment ? | And the main character, what is he like?
Marinette: Attachant. La scène où il ouvre sa boutique après la tempête m'a vraiment émue. | Endearing. The scene where he opens his shop after the storm really moved me.

example: Il a adapté un roman. | He adapted a novel.

[grammar]
grammar: Qui, que, dont and où | Qui replaces the subject, que replaces the direct object, dont replaces de plus noun, and où replaces a place or time. After dont, the subject comes next, with no que.
example: C'est un film que j'ai adoré. | It's a film that I loved.
example: Le film dont tout le monde parle. | The film everyone is talking about.
grammar: Describing a work | To describe a film or book, give the genre, the plot, the characters, and your reaction. Use adjectives that agree: une intrigue captivante, un personnage attachant, des scènes émouvantes.
example: C'est l'histoire d'un boulanger. | It's the story of a baker.
example: J'ai trouvé l'intrigue captivante. | I found the plot gripping.
